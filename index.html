FAQ

Things people usually ask.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you need, so we quote each project after a short conversation. Message us on WhatsApp and we will send a clear, itemised quote.

How long does it take?

A website is much quicker than a full billing system. We agree a timeline before we start and keep you updated while we build.

Is my data safe, and does it belong to me?

Your data is yours. Our billing systems can run on your own computer, work offline and take regular backups.

Do you train my staff and support us after launch?

Yes. We train your team and stay available for fixes, updates and new features.

Can the software be changed to fit how we work?

Yes, that is how we work. Every system is built around your workflow, products and reports.

I am not technical. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Explain your business in plain words and we handle the technical side.
